## Description
`examples/3d/volumetric_fog` still renders the wrong visible scene after the
camera, fog, light, pipeline variant, depth binding, and uniform boundaries
were audited. The capture is dominated by a near gray/white wall or scene
surface, while the checked-in human-reviewed target shows the authored street
scene through the fog-volume frame.

This points at a local scene render-space or visibility parity gap around the
`VolumetricFogExample.glb` scene. This issue is only a grouping issue; the
executable work is split by evidence step and Bevy source-owner boundary.

## Design
Use these child issues as the working sequence:

- ISS-300 identifies the concrete mesh, entity, or render phase item that
  creates the near wall-like frame dominance.
- ISS-301 audits Bevy glTF node and scene import parity for the identified
  contributor.
- ISS-302 audits Bevy scene spawning and transform propagation parity.
- ISS-303 audits Bevy glTF material, culling, and depth-state parity.
- ISS-304 audits Bevy PBR mesh extraction, visibility, and render phase parity.
- ISS-305 applies the source-owner fix once the confirmed divergence is known.

Do not move the camera, hide the wall, edit the glTF asset, change capture
timing, or disable volumetric fog to make the screenshot look plausible.
