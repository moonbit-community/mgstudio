## Description
Bevy's atmosphere scene includes a raw-sunlight directional light with
`VolumetricLight`, a scaled `FogVolume`, and `dynamic_scene` sun rotation
controlled by `GameState.paused`.

## Design
Audit these light/fog/runtime anchors separately from atmosphere camera and
water. Verify that the active frame's sun and fog-volume data reach the
volumetric fog render path.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Directional light illuminance, transform, shadow flag, and
      `VolumetricLight` are classified.
- [ ] FogVolume default value, scale, and translation are classified.
- [ ] `dynamic_scene` sun rotation and pause behavior are classified.
- [ ] Focused evidence proves sun/fog data reaches the volumetric fog pass.
