## Description
Bevy's atmosphere example spawns the terrain glTF and an
`ExtendedMaterial<StandardMaterial, Water>` water plane with a non-sRGB normal
map and exact octave settings.

## Design
Audit `setup_terrain_scene`, `Water`, `WaterSettings`, `MaterialExtension`,
and `spawn_water` against the mgstudio example. Do not replace the Bevy water
path with a simpler material or static reflection.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Terrain asset path, transform, scale, and rotation are classified.
- [ ] Water extended material shader path and normal-map loader settings are
      classified.
- [ ] Water octave vectors, scales, strengths, base material, and transform
      are classified.
- [ ] Any remaining water/terrain divergence is fixed or filed as a narrower
      ISS.
