## Close Notes

Resolved on 2026-07-15: do not adopt ECS-B. Current MoonBit cannot express a
generic valtype constraint, `#valtype` does not guarantee deep immutability, and
raw `MutArrayView` cannot provide tracked non-escaping writes. A restricted
generated schema would add substantial machinery while no longer delivering
the originally proposed general user-defined valtype component model.
