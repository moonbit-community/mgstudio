## Description
The final `examples/3d/volumetric_fog` visual gate still captures a frame
dominated by a camera-adjacent wall or surface after ISS-372 through ISS-375
ruled out volumetric uniforms, light/shadow numeric inputs, and post-pass
composition. Identify the exact remaining Bevy source owner and repair the
mgstudio fragment source-faithfully.

## Design
Start from the current failing capture, not from screenshots alone. Compare the
Bevy `examples/3d/volumetric_fog` scene spawn, glTF scene import, node
transform, primitive/camera-space render extraction, culling, and material
paths against the corresponding mgstudio fragments. Classify every source-shape
anchor touched by the failing frame as complete, facade, blocked, or partial.

Do not tune the camera, edit the asset, alter reference images, add local
fallbacks, or introduce screenshot-specific code. If the exact Bevy behavior
cannot be represented because of a dependency or MoonBit language limitation,
record a separate blocker and stop instead of approximating.

## Close Notes

`Milky2018/meshopt_mbt.generate_tangents(..., compatible=true)` from
`mgstudio-engine/mesh/mesh3d.mbt`.
