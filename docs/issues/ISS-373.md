## Description
Group the smaller diagnostics that prove whether the mesh-view light and
shadow inputs consumed by the real `volumetric_fog` shader variant are
numerically Bevy-equivalent, not merely present and bound.

## Design
Complete the leaf diagnostics in source-owner order:

- ISS-376 maps the Bevy and mgstudio source anchors.
- ISS-377 proves directional and ambient GPU light values.
- ISS-378 proves directional shadow cascade and depth texture values.
- ISS-379 proves clustered point and spot light values.
- ISS-380 records the final light/shadow conclusion and assigns any proven
  repair owner to ISS-375.

Do not repair light extraction, shadow preparation, or shader logic from this
group unless a precise owner is proven by a leaf issue; assign the repair to
ISS-375.

## Close Notes
Closed on 2026-07-05 after ISS-376 through ISS-380 were completed.

Light/shadow numeric-input conclusion:

- Source anchors were mapped in ISS-376.
- Directional and ambient light GPU values were ruled out in ISS-377.
- Directional shadow cascade, texture binding, and sampler binding were ruled
  out in ISS-378 after the local directional comparison sampler source-shape
  repair in ISS-388.
- Clustered point/spot light packing found one real local divergence in
  ISS-379: `GpuClusteredLight` tail fields were written in the wrong order.
  That repair was completed and verified in ISS-379.
- ISS-380 concluded that, after the ISS-379 repair, this light/shadow
  numeric-input group has no known remaining divergence to assign to ISS-375.

No hard external blocker or language limitation was found by this group.
