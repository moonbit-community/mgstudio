## Description
Prove that the real `examples/3d/volumetric_fog` frame applies exposure,
tonemapping, color-space conversion, and final surface copy in the same way as
Bevy after the volumetric fog pass.

## Design
Use the owner map from ISS-381 and pass-order evidence from ISS-383. Compare
the Bevy postprocess source owners against mgstudio for:

- exposure resource and default value;
- tonemapping mode and shader variant;
- HDR-to-surface format conversion;
- final copy or presentation path;
- any example-specific postprocess settings that affect the fog result.

Do not tune constants or insert local color correction.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Bevy exposure, tonemapping, color-space, and final-copy source anchors
      are cited in close notes.
- [ ] The corresponding mgstudio runtime values are asserted or recorded for
      the real example.
- [ ] Any divergence is assigned to ISS-375 with exact owner and field name.
- [ ] If no divergence is found, close with evidence that rules out color and
      final-copy composition.
