## Description
Prove that the real `examples/3d/volumetric_fog` point and spot lights packed
into clustered light GPU data match Bevy's source expressions.

## Design
Use the owner map from ISS-376. Compare Bevy `ClusteredLight` layout and
packing against mgstudio for:

- point and spot light ordering;
- position, radius, range, and inverse-square range;
- color and intensity scaling;
- flags, including volumetric, shadow, and spot-light bits;
- spot direction, inner/outer angle fields, and tangent values;
- shadow depth bias, normal bias, near z, and related shadow indices.

Use focused tests or recorded runtime evidence from the real example after its
setup and tweak systems run.

## Close Notes
Closed on 2026-07-05.

Updated on 2026-07-06 to correct the recorded proof against the current Bevy
source layout.

Bevy source evidence:

- `bevy/crates/bevy_pbr/src/render/light.rs::prepare_lights` sorts point and
  spot lights as clusterable objects, with point lights before spot lights and
  shadow/volumetric-enabled lights first inside those groups.
- The GPU memory layout is defined by
  `bevy/crates/bevy_pbr/src/cluster/mod.rs::GpuClusteredLight`, not by the
  named-field initializer order in `render/light.rs`. The 20-word layout is:
  words 0-3 `light_custom_data`, words 4-7
  `color_inverse_square_range`, words 8-11 `position_radius`, word 12 `flags`,
  word 13 `shadow_depth_bias`, word 14 `shadow_normal_bias`, word 15
  `spot_light_tan_angle`, word 16 `soft_shadow_size`, word 17
  `shadow_map_near_z`, word 18 `decal_index`, and word 19 `range`.
- For point lights, Bevy writes the lower-right 2x2 values of the cube-face
  projection into `light_custom_data` and writes `spot_light_tan_angle = 0.0`.
- For spot lights, Bevy writes direction x/z, spot scale, spot offset, and
  `tan(outer)` using the extracted spot-light angles.
- Bevy flags include shadow maps, contact shadows, volumetric, affects
  lightmapped mesh diffuse, spot-light, and spot-light-y-negative bits.

Mgstudio evidence:

- `mgstudio-engine/pbr/render/light.mbt::render3d_lights_push_clustered_light`
  currently pushes the tail fields as `spot_light_tan_angle`,
  `soft_shadow_size`, `shadow_map_near_z`, `decal_index`, `range`, matching the
  Bevy `GpuClusteredLight` struct layout.
- The 2026-07-06 follow-up did not require production clustered-light code
  changes. It refreshed the `examples/3d/volumetric_fog/main_wbtest.mbt`
  assertions so the real example checks word 15 through word 19 against the
  current Bevy layout.

Verification:

- `moon -C mgstudio-engine test examples/3d/volumetric_fog --target native --release`
  passed with 21/21 tests.

No hard external blocker was found, and no follow-up assignment to ISS-375 is
needed for clustered point/spot light packing.
