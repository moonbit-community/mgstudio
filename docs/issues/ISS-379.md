## Description
Prove that the real `examples/3d/volumetric_fog` point and spot lights packed
into clustered light GPU data match Bevy's source expressions.

## Design
Use the owner map from ISS-376. Compare Bevy `ClusteredLight` layout and
packing against mgstudio for:

- point and spot light ordering;
- position, radius, range, and inverse-square range;
- color and intensity scaling;
- flags, including volumetric, shadow, and spot-light bits;
- spot direction, inner/outer angle fields, and tangent values;
- shadow depth bias, normal bias, near z, and related shadow indices.

Use focused tests or recorded runtime evidence from the real example after its
setup and tweak systems run.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] The Bevy source expression for every in-scope point and spot light field
      is cited in close notes.
- [ ] The corresponding mgstudio packed GPU value is asserted or recorded for
      the real example.
- [ ] Any divergence is assigned to ISS-375 with exact owner and field name.
- [ ] If no divergence is found, close with evidence that rules out clustered
      point and spot light values.
