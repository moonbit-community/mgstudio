## Description

`examples/stress_tests/many_animated_sprites` stopped rendering its sprite
field under the standard native capture path. A row-query build showed only one
center sprite at frame 1 and only the background at frame 90, while
`reference.png` showed the expected field of animated sprites.

The symptom predates and is independent of the ISS-456
`mark_newly_hidden_entities_invisible_system` batch migration: fresh fixed-frame
row and batch frame-90 captures are byte-identical with SHA-256
`8c4e68b35228f8c4473bc86b4ab74c71d94c141ef9478caffd1ac762c3a39afa`.

The owning regression was the stale Camera2d frustum repaired by `e908fa6fb`.
The stress example's sprites were spawned and animated, but the visibility
stage evaluated them against the initial `[-1, 1]` frustum after the camera
moved. A fresh frame-90 capture after that repair again contains the expected
field of animated sprites.

## Design

Retain the Bevy-shaped camera ownership restored by `e908fa6fb`: update
main-world Camera target information, update Camera2d frusta after transform
propagation, and run visibility after the frustum stage. Do not disable culling
or special-case the stress example.

The example source itself still differs from the pinned Bevy baseline in scene
randomization, sprite size, timer initialization, camera movement, window
configuration, and system ordering. That older source-parity work is separated
as ISS-462 rather than mixed into this visibility regression.
